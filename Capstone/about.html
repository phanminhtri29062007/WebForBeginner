About me

I'm a developer and designer based between code editor and sketchbook. Over the past six years I've helped early-stage teams turn rough ideas into interfaces people actually enjoy using — usually starting with a pencil, like this site did.

I care most about the middle distance between engineering and design: systems that are consistent enough to scale and specific enough to feel like someone made them on purpose.
